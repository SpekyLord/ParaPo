# M3 demo results — 2026-11-07

This records a small local class demo using the images the user supplied in chat. It is not a model comparison or an acceptance result. M3-01–04 are complete for this starter set; M3-05–09 are partial, and M3-10 remains undecided.

## Dataset

- 16 images were supplied. Fourteen photos of vehicles/signs are used; poster graphics 06 and 10 are excluded.
- Original image URLs and creator/license details were not supplied. The images, manifests, checkpoints, and exports stay local and ignored by Git. Incidental people and plates are not labels.
- The labels were visually checked against the photos. Clear box errors in images 03, 13, 15, and 16 were corrected. Images 04 and 12 have unreadable text; uncertain transcriptions and route IDs not printed on a sign remain unknown.
- Split: 10 train, 2 validation, 2 test. Images 01 and 02, which may show the same bus, are both in train. A quick exact-hash and 64-bit average-hash check found no exact matches or pairs within 8 bits. Original-source derivatives cannot be traced because the image sources are unknown.

## YOLOv8n run

Ultralytics YOLOv8n 8.4.203 was fine-tuned from its `yolov8n.pt` initialization on the 10 training images. The run used 320-pixel input, batch 2, seed 42, max 20 epochs and patience 5; early stopping ended at epoch 6, with the best checkpoint from epoch 1. Validation had two images. PyTorch 2.11.0+cu128 ran on the PC's NVIDIA RTX 4050 Laptop GPU. EasyOCR 1.7.2 (English) read both ground-truth and detected crops after 3x cubic enlargement.

The checkpoint was trained on v3. The YOLO training and validation label files were byte-for-byte identical in v3 and v4; v4 corrected only a test box and a validation transcription, so the same checkpoint was used for the final report.

Three detections were compared; the other samples had no detections. The detections were false positives, and the export is not treated as phone-ready. Local parity summary: `datasets/m3-samples/runs/demo-v4/parity-result.json`.

No model ran on the phone. The current ADB listing showed the device offline; USB tethering by itself did not make ADB usable. There is no detector inference path in the Android app, and no Android code was changed. Phone latency, memory, FPS, and on-device parity are unmeasured.
