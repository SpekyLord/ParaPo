# Austria — development and handoff

## What you are helping finish

Keep developing, fixing and integrating the demo; the others own their assigned test, review and presentation work.

## Before starting

Have the current normal APK, available permitted samples and recent evidence ready. Agree on a group chat or other shared place for submissions.

## Steps

1. Share the normal demo APK and its exact build identifier; tell the team which Android version/device it needs.
2. Provide permitted sample photos with IDs, existing labels and boxes, current screenshots or recordings, and links to relevant evidence. Give Santotome the prepared 12-photo batch `parapo-review-v11-2026-11-15`, including known problem cases; do not alter frozen manifests.
3. Explain where to send results and ask for build, device, steps, actual behavior and evidence. Plain text, screenshots or a document are fine.
4. Review reproducible reports, fix useful issues, and return an updated build and identifier for targeted retesting. Keep original evidence and submitted corrections separate.
5. Check the team's presentation claims against current behavior and actual evidence. Record real contributions in the [team log](README.md#contribution-log).

## Checklist

- [ ] Share build and materials.
- [ ] Agree on submission location and device access.
- [ ] Review findings and arrange any targeted retests.
- [ ] Check claims and record actual work.

## Send back and completion

Share the handoff in the agreed group chat; put actual integration notes in the team log or a shared document. This is complete when the team has access, submitted findings have a response, needed retests have a build, and presentation claims have been checked.

## Presentation

Explain your actual development and integration work. Be ready to answer:

1. What does the current build support?
2. What changed after a reported issue, if anything?
3. What can the scanner evidence actually establish?
4. What remains unfinished?

## If blocked

Tell the group what is missing and offer a current recording or pairing if a compatible phone is unavailable. Do not invent a build or result.
